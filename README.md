One image: an open passport spread for the Republic of Valdoria, a
country that isn't there. The data page shows holder Elena Marović
(born 14 Jun 1994, Marovel), her portrait, bilingual Valdorian /
English fields, the coat of arms, and a machine-readable zone with
real ICAO check digits. The visas page carries a Tarnovia business
visa — single entry, 28 Mar – 27 Apr 2026, no employment permitted,
40 koruna fee paid — and ten entry and exit stamps that tell one
coherent travel history: Marovel → Velgrad (Tarnovia) → Port Sarn
(Sarnia) → Marovel, then Marovel → Velgrad → Marovel again. Every
stamp is dated after the passport's issue (12 Mar 2026) and no date
contradicts the place of issue.

What's on the spread:

- Data page: Republic of Valdoria header, coat of arms (three peaks,
  star, waves), document No. VD 0482137, portrait, eight bilingual
  fields, holder's signature, issue 12 Mar 2026 / expiry 12 Mar 2036,
  Ministry of the Interior authority stamp, and the two-line MRZ.
- Visas page: Tarnovia visa sticker No. TV 2026 1184 with conditions
  and a consular-fee stamp, plus ten stamps — five circular and five
  rectangular — in four ink colours, each with a place, a direction
  and a date that agrees with the others.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (1024×1024, under 90 KB),
pure standard library, deterministic — every run writes the same bytes.
